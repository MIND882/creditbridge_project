# LEAD MODULE

------

# BUSINESS PROBLEM

BUSINESSES INTERACT WITH HUNDREDS OR EVEN THOUSANDS OF PEOPLE EVERY YEAR.

SOME PEOPLE  BECOME CUSTOMERS.
MOST DO NOT.

BUT WITHOUT A STRUCTURED SYSTEM, BUSINESS LOSE TRACK OF CONVERSTION, FORGET FOLLOW-UP , DUPLICATE EFFORTS, AND MISS REVENUVE OPPORTUNIITES.

A LEAD MANAGEMENT SYSTEM EXISTS TO CUPTURE EVERY POTENTIAL CUSTOMER BEFORE THE BECOME AN ACTUAL CUSTOMER.

A Lead is not a customer.

A Lead is simply a person or organization that has shown some level of interest in the business.

The purpose of the Lead module is to organize, track, qualify, and convert potential customers into paying customers through a structured business process.

---

# Why Does This Module Exist?

The Lead module exists to solve the following business problems:

- Leads get forgotten.
- Follow-ups are missed.
- Customer information becomes scattered across WhatsApp, Email, Phone Calls, and Excel files.
- Multiple employees contact the same person.
- Businesses cannot measure their sales pipeline.
- Management cannot predict future revenue.

The Lead module centralizes every potential customer into one structured system.

---

# Business Goal

The goal of this module is to ensure that every potential customer has a defined lifecycle from first contact until one of the following outcomes:

- Converted into a Customer
- Disqualified
- Lost
- Archived

No lead should disappear without a recorded outcome.

---

# What is a Lead?

A Lead is an individual or organization that has expressed interest in a company's product or service but has not yet become a customer.

Examples:

- Filled a website contact form
- Sent a WhatsApp inquiry
- Called the business
- Walked into a showroom
- Referred by another customer
- Imported from an exhibition
- Generated from a marketing campaign

At this stage there is no confirmed business relationship.

# Primary Business Responsibilities

The Lead module is responsible for:

- Recording every new lead.
- Storing lead information.
- Tracking communication history.
- Recording follow-ups.
- Assigning ownership.
- Managing lead status.
- Supporting lead qualification.
- Maintaining complete lead history.
- Preparing leads for conversion.

---

# What This Module Does NOT Do

The Lead module does not:

- Generate invoices.
- Process payments.
- Manage customer accounts.
- Approve loans.
- Store accounting records.

Its responsibility ends when a Lead is either converted or closed.

---

# Success Criteria

A successful Lead Management System should allow a business to answer questions like:

- How many leads were generated this month?
- Which source produces the highest quality leads?
- Which salesperson converts the most leads?
- How many leads are waiting for follow-up?
- Which leads have been inactive for more than 30 days?
- What is the current conversion rate?
- How many leads became customers?
