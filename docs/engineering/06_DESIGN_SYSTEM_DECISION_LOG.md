# CreditBridge Design Decision Log

---

## Why Indigo?

Because

Financial Trust

Intelligence

Premium Feel

Accessibility

Brand Recognition

Not because it "looks good."

---

## Why Cool Greys?

Warm greys introduce unwanted color shifts.

Cool greys improve contrast.

Cool greys make charts easier to read.

Cool greys work better for financial dashboards.

---

## Why 50–950 Scale?

Compatible with

Tailwind

Material

Radix

Shadcn

Future Dark Mode

Figma Variables

---

## Why AI Purple?

Purple communicates

Machine Intelligence

Generated Content

Computation

without conflicting with

Success

Warning

Error

---

## Why Loan Status Colors are separate?

Loan Status

≠

Risk Score

These represent different domains.

Mixing them creates cognitive overload.

---

## Why Business Logic stays inside Features?

Shared UI must remain reusable.

Business logic changes frequently.

UI changes slowly.

Keeping them separate reduces maintenance.

---

## Why Design Tokens?

Single Source of Truth

Dark Mode

White Label

Theme Switching

Figma Sync

Tailwind Sync

React Sync

Future React Native

All benefit from Tokens.

---

## Why Absolute Imports?

Cleaner code.

No ../../../

Easy refactoring.

Better IDE support.

---

## Why Named Exports?

Tree shaking.

Refactoring safety.

Better autocomplete.

No default export ambiguity.

---

not
